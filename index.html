Your favourite 90s game

It flies, you lift! The phone calls it. You react. Gather around one phone, put your fingers on the screen, and be the last one standing.

How to play

  1. Each player puts one finger on a circle.
  2. Listen to the callout — or read it.
  3. Lift your finger ONLY when it flies!
  4. Wrong lift, or no lift on a flyer — you're out.
  5. Last one standing wins!
No accounts. No ads. Just pure gameplay.
